## The Bootstrap Paradox

The Bootstrap Paradox (or ontological paradox) is a theoretical time travel loop where an object or piece of information has no true origin. It occurs when an item from the future is sent back to the past, becomes the reason it exists in the future, and creates a self-sustaining cycle.

## Caddo Lake 

Caddo Lake is a 2024 American mystery thriller film directed by Celine Held and Logan George and produced by M. Night Shyamalan. Starring Dylan O'Brien and Eliza Scanlen, the story follows the investigation into an 8-year-old girl's mysterious disappearance on Caddo Lake, which connects to past deaths and a shocking, reality-bending family secret.

## Dark

The German sci-fi thriller Dark on Netflix follows four families in a small town named Winden. When a child goes missing, a dark mystery unfolds. The search links the present year to 1986 and 1953 through time travel and a local nuclear power plant.

## What is the purpose of this repository?

I’d like to reinforce some programming concepts through themes and audiovisual works that I enjoy. Both productions address the same subject, but at different levels. In my view, *Caddo Lake* is a good introduction to the Bootstrap paradox, while *Dark* is a more in-depth exploration of the topic. To understand what I’ll be discussing here, I recommend watching one of these two works. Enjoy have a great movie or episode!
